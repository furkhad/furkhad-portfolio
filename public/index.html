02 / HOW I WORK

Curiosity is useful.
Evidence is better.

The goal is not to make a tool look sophisticated. It is to make its behavior understandable, its limits visible, and its results testable.

A / 01

Observe first

Start with system behavior, logs, traffic, and the available evidence before choosing a theory.

A / 02
✓

Verify the result

Prefer reproducible checks, measurable outcomes, and clear limitations over unsupported claims.

A / 03
↗

Build responsibly

Keep scope explicit, respect authorization boundaries, and design for useful human decisions.
